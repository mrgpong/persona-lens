What's real vs. simulated in this build
Live in this demo

Camera preview, microphone capture and the reactive waveform use your actual device hardware via getUserMedia and AnalyserNode. The voice enrollment clip is really recorded.

Simulated here

Face matching, live transcription and AI summaries are scripted for this preview. In production these map to POST /recognize, POST /process-interaction and the Ollama qwen3:8b call.

Diarization

Host vs. other speaker segments are illustrative. The real path enrolls a pyannote.audio voice print once, then diarizes every session against that fixed profile.

Persisted locally

People, sessions and your enrollment status save to this browser only, so the demo behaves consistently across visits without a backend.
